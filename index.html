Welcome to ninefives.cloud

Cloud hosting for things that aren't that important.

Wait, 9-5s?

Yes.

Why would anyone use this?

Your personal blog gets a few visitors a month. Your latest vibe coded side-project doesn't need to be globally distributed. Your Minecraft server for you and your two friends doesn't need enterprise SLA guarantees.

What do we even offer?

Many of the cloud primitives you'd expect:

  • Compute
  • Block Storage
  • Ephemeral Storage
  • Object Storage
  • Serverless Functions
  • Managed Redis
  • Managed Kafka
  • Inference API (soon)

What's our infrastructure?

A stack of old hardware that I've collected over time and Frankenstein'd into something useful. There's an old battery backup that has enough juice to keep things online for five minutes when the power goes out. And now we have a backup connection through Starlink, so that's neat.
